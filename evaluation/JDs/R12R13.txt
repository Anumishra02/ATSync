Description
As a product designer on our team, you'll design intuitive interfaces and collaborate closely with engineers, data scientists, and manufacturing experts to transform complex manufacturing problems into user-friendly experiences that drive operational excellence across Apple's global supply chain.

Work closely with manufacturing teams and stakeholders to learn about their daily workflows and understand what they need to do their jobs effectively

Share design insights and ideas with product and engineering teams to help improve our platforms and user experiences

Build interactive prototypes that accurately represent data and functionality, helping teams visualize new features across our different platforms

Test designs with real users to make sure they're intuitive and solve the right problems for people who use our tools every day

Create detailed design handoffs and work alongside engineering teams to bring designs to life, ensuring they work well for our users

Minimum Qualifications
Bachelor's degree in related field, or equivalent professional experience
1+ years of experience in UX/UI design with a portfolio demonstrating user-centered design process
Proficiency in design tools such as Sketch, Figma, or Adobe Creative Suite
Strong communication skills and ability to present design rationale to stakeholders
Ability to work collaboratively within cross-functional teams, including engineers and product managers
Experience with user research methods specific to enterprise, data-driven products

Preferred Qualifications
Advanced degree in a relevant field or 2+ years of professional experience
Familiarity with information visualization, data storytelling, or data journalism
Basic understanding of data visualization libraries and frameworks (D3, Observable, Tableau, etc.)
Exposure to statistical analysis and data manipulation or tools (SQL, Python pandas, R, etc.)
Working knowledge of web technologies (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) and an interest in prototyping interactive visualizations
